Meet your tarot reader

Meet Divine Monica Harsh

Divine Monica Harsh has spent over 20 years working in tarot reading and spiritual guidance, helping people explore personal, professional and relationship-related questions.

Her journey into tarot began with a deep interest in spiritual studies and led her to formal learning and training in Rajasthan. Today, she offers personalised tarot readings for individuals navigating relationships, career and important life decisions.

Her approach focuses on understanding the symbolism of the cards, listening carefully to each person's concerns and exploring possible paths forward in a supportive, confidential setting.
