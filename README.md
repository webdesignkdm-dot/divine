# Divine Monica Harsh — 3-Day Tarot Webinar

Open index.html in a browser. The page uses Bootstrap 5 from jsDelivr, Google Fonts, and the supplied reference imagery cropped into local files under assets/.

## Before publishing

- Add the confirmed month and year to the hero note and the first FAQ answer.
- Add the Razorpay payment link to RAZORPAY_PAYMENT_URL near the end of index.html. With this set, a valid form submission redirects the visitor to that link. The static page does not store or send registration form details.
- Add direct video file links (.mp4) or YouTube watch/share links to TESTIMONIAL_VIDEO_URLS. The video slider and modal are already wired to those slots.
- Both the video and written review sliders show three cards at once on desktop, then advance automatically and loop. They adapt to two cards on tablet and one on mobile; arrows and dots are also available.
